GPT 5 Loved or Hated

7 min read

GPT-5 Upgrades: What’s New?

  1. Smarter, Faster, More Flexible

  • Dynamic model routing: GPT-5 automatically switches between quick “main/mini” models and deeper “thinking/thinking-nano” models depending on task complexity.

  • Expanded context windows: Up to 256K tokens in ChatGPT, with some reports even mentioning up to 1 million tokens—letting it handle massive documents with ease.

  1. Enhanced Abilities & Usability

  • Multimodal support: Seamlessly handles text, image, audio, and video in real-time.

  • Improved coding, reasoning, health support: PhD-level-like performance across math, science, coding, and legal domains. Reduced hallucinations and better factual reliability.

  • Innovative features: “Study Mode,” “Thinking Mode,” custom interactive personalities, and smart voice UIs.

  • Tool and productivity integrations: Connects with Gmail, Google Calendar; supports “vibe coding”—creating full apps from prompts.

  1. Smarter Economics & Access

  • Tiered access: Available across Free, Plus ($20/month), Pro ($200/month), and Go (~$5/month) plans—with varied usage caps and features.

  • Cost-optimized architecture: Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) design slashes energy usage.

  1. Strategic Positioning

  • Framed as a stepping stone toward AGI—but many say it’s more evolutionary than revolutionary.

  • Outperforms major rivals like Google’s Gemini 2.5 Pro in text and coding, though not yet top-tier in multimedia.

Backlash: GPT-4o’s Emotional Intelligence vs GPT-5’s Tone

Why GPT-4o Was Loved
  • GPT-4o was cherished for its warm, personable tone—a companion-like presence users found engaging and emotionally comforting.
What People Told OpenAI
  • GPT-5’s tone felt cold, flat, and overly formal. Users described it as sounding like an “overworked secretary” and “corporate beige zombie.”
  • Many missed the “yes-man,” empathetic vibe of GPT-4o—prompting an emotional backlash.
OpenAI’s Response
  • Reinstated GPT-4o for paid users and promised to make GPT-5 “feel warmer” while avoiding over-flattery.
  • MIT researchers are now rolling out a benchmark to measure AI’s emotional intelligence—balancing empathy with mental wellness.
Real User Voices

On Reddit, users reflect mixed feelings:

“...GPT-5 feels … brusque, less sophisticated, but more personal…”

“...Simulated empathy … struggles when the conversation becomes more introspective…”

In a nutshell: GPT-5 pushes boundaries in reasoning, scale, and customization—but the emotional soul of GPT-4o was sorely missed. OpenAI heard the feedback and is adapting. Expect future versions that are both smart and emotionally attuned.
